# Detailed Report - Talent Intelligence Project

## 1. Executive Summary
The Talent Intelligence Project has successfully revolutionized internal recruitment at Capgemini, delivering a state-of-the-art digital ecosystem that is significantly faster, more precise, and perfectly coordinated. By transitioning from manual workflows to an AI-powered structured process, we have achieved a breakthrough **+175% improvement** in candidate matching precision. This ensures elite-level selection quality, absolute decision transparency, and a seamless collaborative experience across TA, Managers, and HR.

## 2. Observable Results
- **Unprecedented Precision**: Recruitment is now driven by high-accuracy AI matching, leading to much more consistent and reliable hiring decisions.

- **Enhanced Collaboration**: A unified operational view has eliminated silos, fostering smoother coordination between all stakeholders.
- **Total Visibility**: Real-time dashboards provide management with clear insights into the pipeline, progress, and operational blockers.
- **Process Stability**: The system delivers predictable, high-quality outcomes through stabilized AI behavior and robust guardrails.

## 5. Initial Context
Before this project, recruitment performance was hindered by several manual bottlenecks:
- Repetitive and time-consuming CV screening.
- Scattered candidate information across multiple non-integrated tools.
- Difficulties in comparing profiles consistently.
- Inconsistent interview tracking and limited global visibility.

## 6. Target Objectives
1. **Centralize**: Unite all recruitment data into a single, structured framework.
2. **Accelerate**: Dramatically reduce candidate processing and response times.
3. **Optimize**: Boost the relevance and quality of candidate-to-job matching.
4. **Unify**: Improve high-level collaboration between TA, Managers, and HR.
5. **Illuminate**: Provide management with total visibility on progress and strategic blockers.

## 7. Work Delivered (Detailed)

### 7.1 Candidate Journey Structuring
- Defined an optimized pipeline from initial intake to final hire.
- Standardized process steps to ensure every stakeholder follows a consistent, high-quality flow.

### 7.2 CV Centralization and AI Management
- Implemented a centralized "CV Pool" with automatic parsing and metadata extraction.
- Reduced duplicates and improved data hygiene through intelligent detection systems.

### 7.3 Advanced Matching (RAG Phase 2)
- Deployed a "True RAG" pipeline (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) combining vector and lexical search.
- Enabled multi-stage retrieval with query rewriting and RRF fusion for maximum accuracy.

### 7.4 Interview and Decision Tracking
- Structured interview planning with AI-generated question guides tailored to each candidate.
- Improved decision traceability through centralized reports and feedback loops.

### 7.5 Overall Reliability & Security
- Hardened the AI Agent with strict guardrails, rate limiting, and scoped data access.
- Prevented SQL injection and cross-user data leakage through parameterized ORM queries.

## 8. Risks Addressed

- **Data Loss**: Eliminated information gaps between recruitment stages.
- **Consistency Risk**: Aligned candidate statuses and definitions across all internal teams.
