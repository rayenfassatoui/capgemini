# Detailed Report - Talent Intelligence Project

## 1. Executive Summary
The objective of this project is to transform internal recruitment into a faster, more reliable, and better coordinated process across teams. The work delivered moved recruitment from a mostly manual workflow to a structured process with better visibility, higher candidate selection quality, and stronger decision tracking.

## 2. Initial Context
Before this project, several issues were slowing down recruitment performance:
- CV screening was long and repetitive.
- Candidate information was scattered.
- Profile comparison was difficult and time-consuming.
- Interview tracking was sometimes inconsistent across teams.
- There was limited global visibility on recruitment progress.

## 3. Target Objectives
1. Centralize all recruitment information in one framework.
2. Reduce candidate processing time.
3. Improve the relevance of candidate-to-job preselection.
4. Improve collaboration between TA, managers, and HR.
5. Provide clear visibility on progress and blockers.

## 4. Work Delivered (Detailed)

### 4.1 Candidate Journey Structuring
- Defined a clear pipeline from CV intake to final hiring decision.
- Standardized process steps so each stakeholder follows the same flow.
- Clarified role responsibilities to avoid ownership gaps.

### 4.2 CV Centralization and Management
- Implemented a single space for candidate management.
- Organized candidate information to make reading and comparison easier.
- Reduced duplicate records and improved data hygiene.

### 4.3 Matching Quality Improvement
- Improved the quality of matching between job needs and candidate profiles.
- Enabled more reliable prioritization of high-value profiles.
- Increased consistency between business expectations and selected candidates.

### 4.4 Interview and Decision Tracking
- Structured interview planning and scheduling.
- Improved tracking of feedback and interview reports.
- Increased decision traceability at each stage.

### 4.5 Cross-Team Coordination
- Established a shared operational view across TA, managers, and HR.
- Aligned candidate statuses to reduce misunderstandings.
- Improved information flow between stakeholders.

### 4.6 Management Visibility and Steering
- Consolidated useful tracking indicators for each role.
- Improved prioritization of recruitment actions.
- Improved the ability to identify operational blockers quickly.

### 4.7 Overall Reliability Reinforcement
- Stabilized overall behavior to reduce unpredictable outcomes.
- Improved control over result quality.
- Validated improvements with concrete measurements.

## 5. Observable Results
- Recruitment process is faster and better organized.
- Time savings on screening and follow-up activities.
- Improved preselection quality.
- More consistent decisions based on better structured information.
- Smoother collaboration between teams.

## 6. Key Figures (Internal Evaluation)
- Evaluation campaign on 40 real cases.
- Reference coverage: 85%.
- Precision improvement up to +175% depending on the measured indicator.
- Error rate maintained at 0% during evaluation.

## 7. Business Impact by Population

### Talent Acquisition (TA)
- Faster sourcing and screening phase.
- Better prioritization of candidates to process.

### Managers
- More relevant candidate profiles received.
- Easier decision making thanks to clearer tracking.

### HR
- More reliable visibility of the overall pipeline.
- Better control of final decision and follow-up stages.

### Administration
- Cross-functional visibility of recruitment activity.
- Strong foundation for governance, reporting, and continuous improvement.

## 8. Risks Addressed
- Reduced errors related to manual processing.
- Limited information loss between stages.
- Reduced status inconsistencies across teams.
